# Prompt Notes

Level and detail prompts live in `backend/prompts.py`. Tune with real
document chunks from your target material before expanding features.

Watch for:
- Primary vs public being indistinguishable (both say "no jargon") -
  differentiate by audience intent, not just vocabulary.
- Deep-dive drifting into a lecture - cap at 8 bullets.
- Equations dropped at university level - test with math-heavy PDFs.
